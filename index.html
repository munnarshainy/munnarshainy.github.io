About

Research background,
industry ambitions.

I'm a psychology graduate with over four years of hands-on experience across EEG, brain-computer interfaces, psychophysiology, and multi-lab collaborative research. I've worked across Bengaluru, Stanford, and large international consortia — and I'm now looking to take that experience into a product or research role at a company that's building something meaningful in neurotech, healthtech, or mental health.

Most recently I was a Research Assistant at Sabi during their stealth-mode phase, where I contributed to early-stage research. Before that I spent three years at Axxonet Brain Research Laboratory learning EEG methodology end-to-end — from study design and data collection to neurofeedback and brain function therapy (BFT). I also contributed remotely to Stanford's Psychophysiology Lab (Dr. James J. Gross) under Kate Petrova, and to the large-scale ManyBabies4 Consortium.

I care about rigour, open science, and building things that are humane by design. I'm comfortable working across research, community, and communication — and I'm particularly drawn to early-stage teams where those lines blur.

Outside work I advocate for diversity and inclusion, mental health awareness, and queer rights. I draw portraits and am slowly working through a reading list.
